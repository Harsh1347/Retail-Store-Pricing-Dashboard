# 📊 Power BI Dashboard: Retail Revenue & Profit Analysis for Price Optimization

## 🔍 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard is an interactive data analytics solution built to visualize, monitor, and evaluate retail sales performance across product categories, cities, and time periods. Designed as a complement to a larger price elasticity optimization initiative, this dashboard focuses on descriptive and diagnostic analytics to uncover patterns in revenue, profit, and unit sales — enabling informed pricing and business decisions.

## 🎯 Key Objectives

- Visualize revenue and profit trends across stores, products, and time.
- Identify top-performing products and locations.
- Provide city-level and category-level breakdowns for business insights.
- Support strategic decisions around pricing, inventory, and marketing.

## 📁 Dataset Summary

The underlying dataset represents transaction data from **Maven Toys**, a fictional retail chain operating in Mexico. It includes:
- Product-level revenue, profit, and unit sales.
- Store-level and city-level performance.
- Monthly data spanning from January 2022 to September 2023.
- Product category metadata and price variations.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 📌 Metrics Summary
- **35** SKUs
- **29** Stores
- **$14.3M** Total Revenue
- **$3.83M** Total Profit

### 🌍 City-Level Insights
- Top cities by revenue: **Ciudad de Mexico**, **Guadalajara**, **Monterrey**.
- Interactive maps highlighting geographic performance differences.

### 🛍️ Category & Product Breakdown
- Revenue split across categories: **Toys**, **Art & Crafts**, **Electronics**, **Games**, **Sports & Outdoors**.
- Top 10 products by revenue and profit (e.g., *Lego Bricks*, *Colorbuds*, *Action Figures*).

### 📅 Time-Series Analysis
- Monthly trends in revenue, profit, and average price.
- Units sold and price changes tracked across time.

### 🧩 Store Performance
- Individual dashboards for stores (e.g., *Maven Toys Aguascalientes*), showcasing local revenue, profit, and sales.

## 📉 Business Insights

- Products like *Colorbuds* and *Lego Bricks* contribute significantly to revenue with minimal elasticity, making them ideal candidates for price increases.
- Cities like *Ciudad de Mexico* dominate in sales, suggesting regional marketing strategies.
- Seasonality and product category impact performance, supporting differentiated pricing.
